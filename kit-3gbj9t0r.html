How to get started

  1. Get your free credit reports from all three bureaus at AnnualCreditReport.com.
  2. Mark anything that is wrong, outdated or that you don't recognize.
  3. Open the guides in the kit first, then pick the letter template that fits each item.
  4. Send letters by certified mail and keep copies of everything you send and receive.
  5. Bureaus generally have 30 days to investigate. Track your dates.
