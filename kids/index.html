📍 About Kids' Financialverse!

Coinbridge Elementary is a school in Ledger Falls — a neighborhood in the town of Money City, where the great Financialverse heroes and villains shape everyday life. Kids at Coinbridge don't always know the names of the forces around them, but they learn, one choice at a time, how to think before they spend.

The Iron Banker watches over Ledger Falls — not by fighting, but by teaching. Every story ends with someone understanding something they didn't before.

More stories coming soon! Tyrone, Sofia, Wei, Aiyana, and many more kids at Coinbridge Elementary are waiting to share their adventures.
