🐷 Penny and the Piggy Bank

Starring Penny (age 7) · For ages 4 and up
💡 Today's Lesson: Saving toward a goal
🏪
Page 1
In the town of Financialverse, there lived a girl named Penny.
Penny"One day... that dragon will be MINE."
🪙
Page 2
Every week, Penny earned one dollar for doing her chores. The dragon cost ten. That meant ten whole weeks of saving.
Penny"Ten weeks is FOREVER."
🧹
Page 3
But Penny got to work.
Penny's piggy bank label"Dragon Fund — Week 1 ✓"
🍦
Page 4
Along the way, there were... temptations.
Friend"Come on, Penny! Ice cream is only a dollar!"
Penny (thinking)"One dollar. That's one whole week of saving..."
🐉
Page 5
Penny thought about the dragon. And she kept walking.
Penny"Not today."
📅
Page 6
Week after week, Penny saved. Some weeks were easy. Some weeks were hard. But every week, the piggy bank got a little heavier.
💰
Page 7
Then one Saturday morning...
Penny"TEN DOLLARS! I DID IT!"
🎉
Page 8
Saving had felt hard. Waiting had felt hard. But holding something she had truly earned?
"That felt like a superpower." 🐉🪙
