Letter templates (43 files)

The kit is organized by situation. Some letters include separate Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion versions; not every letter has a version for all three bureaus.

  • Credit bureau dispute letters (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion versions)
  • Legal follow-up dispute letters (all three bureaus)
  • Legal credit dispute forms (all three bureaus)
  • Sworn denial and basic sworn denial
  • Debt validation letters, including versions used after a credit-report entry or a collection letter
  • Collection dispute letters
  • Collection fax letters (bureau versions and a collectors version)
  • Method of Verification letters — versions A, B, and a “nice” version (all three bureaus)
  • No-response letters (all three bureaus and the original creditor)
  • Dispute letters to the original creditor, including versions for a 30-day late and a re-aged debt
  • Notice of legal action to a collection company
  • Motion to vacate judgment (a court-form template only; this is not legal representation and does not start a court case for you)
  • Payment refusal letter
